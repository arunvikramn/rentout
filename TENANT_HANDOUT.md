# Tenant Handout: Rent & Bills Portal

Welcome to the Rentout portal! This app helps you track your rent, electricity, and water bills in one place.

## 1. Accessing Your Portal
- You will receive a unique link for your unit.
- Keep this link private; it is your personal dashboard.

## 2. Viewing Your Status
- **Rent:** Shows the current month's rent and any pending dues.
- **Bills:** Displays the latest Electricity (KSEB) and Water (KWA) bills fetched automatically from the provider.

## 3. Paying Your Rent
- **Rent Payment:** Pay your rent to the owner as per your agreement.
- **Reporting Payment:** Once paid, click the "Mark Paid" button on your portal.
- **UTR Number:** You **must** enter the UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) from your bank transfer. This is required for verification.
- **Grace Period:** Rent is due on the 1st of every month. Please pay within 3 days (by the 4th) to avoid an automated late fee of ₹100 per day starting from the 5th.

## 4. Paying Your Bills
- **Direct Payment:** Electricity bills (KSEB) should be paid directly to the provider.
- **Water Bills:** Water (KWA) bills are split equally (50%) between both units and should be paid to the owner along with your rent.
- **Updating the Portal:** After paying a bill, you can mark it as paid on the portal for your own reference and records.

## 5. Rent Escalation
- Your rent will automatically increase annually based on your agreement (typically 5% or a minimum of ₹1000, whichever is higher). The portal will reflect this change when it occurs.
